About JingCarbon Future

A composite manufacturer run by engineers — building carbon fiber solutions for customers who need parts that work, delivered on time.

Who We Are

From Dezhou, Shandong to the World

JingCarbon Future (Shandong) New Materials Technology Co., Ltd. is a carbon fiber products manufacturer based in Dezhou, Shandong Province. We cover the full production chain — prepreg lamination, autoclave molding, precision CNC machining and quality inspection — so customers deal with one accountable partner from drawing to delivery.

Our focus: carbon fiber sheets (T300/T700, 3K twill & plain), round and square tubes, and custom structural components for UAV, new energy vehicle and industrial equipment programs.
