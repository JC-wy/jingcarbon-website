Carbon Fiber Sheets

  • 3K twill & plain weave, glossy or matte surface
  • T300 and T700 fiber grades, custom layup on request
  • Custom cutting, drilling and CNC profiling available

Product 02 / Carbon Fiber Tubes

Carbon Fiber Tubes & Profiles

Round, square and rectangular tubes with high bending stiffness and low weight — roll-wrapped or filament-wound to your exact dimensions. Inner and outer surfaces are finished for direct assembly, with optional CNC end-machining for precise fitments and joints.

  • Round, square, rectangular and custom profiles
  • Wall thickness from 0.5 mm, custom ID/OD combinations
  • End machining: threading, slots, precision bores
  • Matte or glossy 3K twill / plain outer finish

Product 03 / Custom Parts & CNC

Custom Parts & 5-Axis CNC

From your 3D model to finished component: autoclave molding, precision 5-axis machining and quality inspection — for UAV, automotive and industrial programs.

UAV Frames

Drone airframes, arm joints, motor mounts and gimbal structures — lightweight, rigid and vibration-tuned.

Automotive Components

Engine covers, battery enclosure panels and structural brackets for new energy vehicles, finished to Class-A surfaces.

Equipment Structures

Machine frames, adapter molds and thin-wall cylinders for precision equipment — machined from solid laminate or molded to net shape.

5-Axis CNC Machining

Complex contours, pockets, undercuts and engravings in one setup — tolerance held to ±0.05 mm.

Secondary Operations

Precision drilling, hole threading, surface polishing, clear coating and custom logos on request.

Prototype to Volume

Fast prototype iterations before tooling commitment, then scale to batch production with inspection reports.
